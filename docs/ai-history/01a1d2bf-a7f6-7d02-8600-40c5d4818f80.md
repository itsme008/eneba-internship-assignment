## 1. Product Overview

**Required:** Develop a web-based digital marketplace inspired by Eneba. Visitors should be able to discover products, compare offers from multiple sellers, and purchase digital products or keys through a checkout process. Payments may be simulated; no real money is involved.

**Proposed Product Statement:** Create a compact, realistic marketplace demo where buyers can complete the journey from product discovery to receiving a simulated digital key, with transparent seller offers and responsive, Eneba-inspired styling.

## 2. Problem and Goal

**User Problem (Proposed):** Buyers need a straightforward way to discover video games and gaming-related products, compare prices from different sellers, and complete purchases through a convenient and reliable shopping experience.

**Product Goal (Proposed):** Deliver a functional marketplace demonstrating product discovery, seller comparison, user authentication, and an end-to-end purchasing experience, including simulated payment and digital key delivery.

## 3. Target Users

The marketplace will support two primary user types: **Visitors (Unauthenticated Users)** and **Registered Buyers (Authenticated Users)**.

### 3.1 Visitors (Unauthenticated Users)

Visitors can explore the marketplace without creating an account.

They can:

- Browse products through the homepage and product catalogue.
- Search for products by name.
- Filter and sort products based on available criteria.
- View individual product details.
- Compare prices and offers from different sellers.
- Register or sign in to access purchasing features.

Visitors cannot complete purchases or access order history without authentication.

### 3.2 Registered Buyers (Authenticated Users)

Registered buyers have access to all visitor functionality, along with additional account and purchasing features.

They can:

- Sign in and sign out of their accounts.
- Manage basic account information.
- Add products to their shopping cart.
- Complete purchases using a simulated payment process.
- Receive and view purchased digital keys.
- Access their order history and purchase details.

### 3.3 Out of Scope

Seller accounts, seller dashboards, and administrator dashboards will not be implemented in the initial version.

Seller profiles and product offers will be maintained through the backend and database to demonstrate marketplace functionality.
